# Before and after examples

Eight transformations across SaaS, AI tools, local-first apps, downloads, dashboards, and docs.

## Example 1: Client-side file utility (60 words to 6 words)

**Before:**
> **🚀 100% Private Browser Storage — Local by Design!**
> Not just another file converter, it's your privacy shield. Your merged PDFs and settings stay inside your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) and IndexedDB — zero files ever leave your computer — saving hosting costs through cutting-edge client-side WebAssembly with no backend server!

**After:**
> **Landing hero:** `Merge PDFs in your browser.`
> **Tool settings footer:** `Clear saved data`

**What changed:** Removed `localStorage`, `IndexedDB`, `WebAssembly`, hosting-cost excuses, `"never leave your computer"` paranoia, the `"Not just..."` setup, em dashes, emoji, and `cutting-edge`.
